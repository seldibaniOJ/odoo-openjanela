Quote windows and doors from Odoo — with a real configurator behind it

OpenJanela adds the window-and-door layer Odoo doesn't have: a rules-based product configurator with instant pricing, generated shop drawings, bills of material, cut lists and optimization, label printing, and shop-floor tracking down to the station scan. It works with your Odoo 19: quote a configured window from the Odoo sales order, and the price, drawing and BOM come back into Odoo. Production dates, stock and shipping stay in sync.

What you get in this module

  • An OpenJanela app in your Odoo apps menu — one click to your OpenJanela server
  • One setting: the OpenJanela URL (hosted or self-hosted)
  • No tables, no fields on your records, no business logic — safe on any database

The full connection

The complete integration is configured from the OpenJanela side through the standard Odoo 19 JSON-2 API — nothing else installs inside Odoo. Start from the Connect OpenJanela to Odoo menu entry, or visit openjanelaerp.com/openjanela-for-odoo.

Free and open

This module is LGPL-3 and free. OpenJanela's core is free to self-host — source download included — with paid modules and hosted options available.
